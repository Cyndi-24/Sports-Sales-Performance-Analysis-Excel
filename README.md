## Project Overview

This project analyses sports retail sales data from 2011 to 2016 using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis brings together revenue, profit, orders, products, subcategories, and geographic performance in an interactive dashboard designed to provide a clear view of overall sales performance.

## Business Problem

Management needed a clearer understanding of how the business was performing across products, markets, and time.

Although sales data was available, it was difficult to quickly identify which markets and products were driving results, how revenue and profit were changing over time, and where management attention should be focused.

The analysis was therefore designed to provide a consolidated view of business performance and highlight the areas contributing most to sales and profitability.

## Business Questions

1. How did revenue and profit change from 2011 to 2016?
2. Which countries generated the most revenue?
3. Which product categories were driving sales in the highest-revenue market?
4. Which subcategories recorded the highest order volume?
5. Which products generated the most revenue?
6. What was the overall business performance in terms of revenue, profit, orders, quantity sold, and average order value?

 ## Data Source

The dataset used for this analysis was obtained from Kaggle and contains sports retail sales transactions covering the period from 2011 to 2016.

The data includes information on orders, revenue, profit, products, product categories and subcategories, customers, and geographic markets.

 ## Data Preparation

Before building the dashboard, the dataset was reviewed and prepared in Excel to improve consistency and reliability.

Key preparation steps included:

- Checked the dataset for missing values and handled incomplete records where necessary.
- Identified and removed duplicate records.
- Corrected typographical and inconsistent text entries.
- Standardised data types and number formats across relevant fields.
- Used Excel formulas to support data validation and transformation.
- Applied lookup functions such as VLOOKUP where information needed to be matched across fields.
- Used filtering, sorting, and grouping to inspect the dataset and identify inconsistencies.
- Verified key sales fields before creating PivotTables and summary calculations.
- Prepared the cleaned dataset for analysis through PivotTables, charts, and dashboard KPIs. 

## Analysis & Visualisation

The cleaned data was summarised using PivotTables and visualised through an interactive Excel dashboard.

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks five core business metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Order Value
- Total Orders

### Dashboard Visuals

The dashboard includes:

- **Revenue and Profit Trend** — tracks changes in revenue and profit from 2011 to 2016.
- **Revenue Generated by Country** — compares revenue performance across geographic markets.
- **Product Category Breakdown – Australia** — examines the product-category mix within the highest-revenue market.
- **Top Subcategories by Order Volume** — identifies the subcategories generating the highest number of orders.
- **Top 5 Products by Revenue** — highlights the products contributing the most revenue.

Interactive slicers allow the dashboard to be filtered across relevant product, customer, and geographic dimensions.

### 1. How did revenue and profit change from 2011 to 2016?

**Finding:** Revenue increased from about **$10.1M in 2011** to a peak of **$22.6M in 2014**. It remained relatively high in 2015 at **$22.3M**, before declining to **$19.6M in 2016**. Profit followed a similar pattern, rising from about **$4.0M in 2011** to roughly **$8.9M in 2014**, then declining to about **$7.5M in 2016**.

**Interpretation:** The business experienced strong growth between 2011 and 2014, with both revenue and profit more than doubling. Performance began to soften after 2014, particularly in 2016, suggesting that the factors supporting earlier growth may have weakened and should be investigated further.

  

  ### 2. Which countries generated the most revenue?

**Finding:** Australia generated the highest revenue, followed closely by the United States.

**Interpretation:** Revenue was concentrated in a few key markets, making Australia and the United States important markets for continued business focus.

### 3. Which product categories were driving sales in the highest-revenue market?

**Finding:** Accessories accounted for the largest share of product-category activity in Australia, followed by Bikes and Clothing.

**Interpretation:** Sales activity in the strongest market was concentrated mainly in Accessories.
